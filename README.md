# GMM Grokbot skills

These skills get better over time as the GMM advanced team improves them. Check back for the latest versions.

Ready-to-use Grokbot skills from the GrokBot Money Models Skool courses. Each folder holds one `SKILL.md`. Copy it into your bot, fill in the [brackets] with your own details, and test on a small safe example first.


## Build Your Grokbot Team (N1)

| Lesson | Skill | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| N1-1 | [GMM Chief of Staff](https://www.github.com/sargshep/gmm-grokbot-skills/tree/main/gmm-chief-of-staff) | Main agent. Routes work to the other bots, keeps the plan, asks me before anything goes out. |
| N1-2 | [GMM Day-one brief](https://www.github.com/sargshep/gmm-grokbot-skills/tree/main/gmm-day-one-brief) | Paste as the first message to your main agent. |
| N1-3 | [GMM Business Profile](https://www.github.com/sargshep/gmm-grokbot-skills/tree/main/gmm-business-profile) | Who we are, what we sell and how we sound. Use whenever writing anything customer-facing. |
| N1-4 | [GMM Skill template](https://www.github.com/sargshep/gmm-grokbot-skills/tree/main/gmm-skill-template) | Fill in the brackets, then ask your bot to save it as a skill. |
| N1-5 | [GMM Bot definition template](https://www.github.com/sargshep/gmm-grokbot-skills/tree/main/gmm-bot-definition-template) | One job per bot. Copy and fill in. |
| N1-6 | [GMM Copy Review](https://www.github.com/sargshep/gmm-grokbot-skills/tree/main/gmm-copy-review) | Reviews any copy against our rules and returns before, after and why. |
| N1-7 | [GMM Routine instruction](https://www.github.com/sargshep/gmm-grokbot-skills/tree/main/gmm-routine-instruction) | Paste into the owning bot and change the details. |
| N1-8 | [GMM Access checklist](https://www.github.com/sargshep/gmm-grokbot-skills/tree/main/gmm-access-checklist) | What this bot may touch. |
| N1-9 | [GMM Safety block](https://www.github.com/sargshep/gmm-grokbot-skills/tree/main/gmm-safety-block) | Standing rules for any bot. |
