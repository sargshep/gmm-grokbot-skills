From the GrokBot Money Models course **Build Your Grokbot Team**, lesson N1-3 "Teach it your business so it stops being generic".

Who we are, what we sell and how we sound. Use whenever writing anything customer-facing.

## How to use
1. Copy the instructions below into your bot (or ask your bot to save them as a skill).
2. Fill in every [bracket] with your own details.
3. Test it on a small, safe example before you switch on any routine.

## Instructions
```
WHEN TO USE: any customer-facing writing or decision about offers.
WHO WE SERVE: [one line]
WHAT WE SELL: [offers and prices, one line each]
TONE: friendly, plain words, short. [your own tone notes]
NEVER SAY: [words, promises, claims]
APPROVAL: customer contact always needs my OK.
```
