From the GrokBot Money Models course **Business Workflows on Autopilot**, lesson N2-9 "The nightly checker".

Looks and reports each morning. Fixes nothing.

## How to use
1. Copy the instructions below into your bot (or ask your bot to save them as a skill).
2. Fill in every [bracket] with your own details.
3. Test it on a small, safe example before you switch on any routine.

## Instructions
```
Each run, check: [1. website and contact form work], [2. every enquiry in the last 24 hours got a reply], [3. offers on the site match the Offer Sheet], [4. open quotes older than 7 days].
RULES: do not fix anything, change any data or place a real order; only look and report. Report only what you saw. If a check could not run, say so as its own line.
REPORT FORMAT: Morning check, <date>: <n> passed, <n> issues. Issues first, numbered: what is wrong, where, expected vs what happened, who should own the fix. Then passed checks, one line each.
```

## Routines
Every day 6:00 AM [time zone].

## Connectors
Website (browser), inbox (read), sheets (read).
