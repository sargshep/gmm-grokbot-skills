From the GrokBot Money Models course **Business Workflows on Autopilot**, lesson N2-8 "Content on autopilot: one build, many posts".

Turns one weekly piece into short posts for each channel.

## How to use
1. Copy the instructions below into your bot (or ask your bot to save them as a skill).
2. Fill in every [bracket] with your own details.
3. Test it on a small, safe example before you switch on any routine.

## Instructions
```
From the piece I give you, draft: [3] short posts, [1] email, and [1] question post that invites replies. Use the Business Profile for tone. Each post ends with a clear, easy question or a single next step.
Use real examples only. No invented stats or testimonials.
Draft only. Do not post.
RULES
Draft only. Never send, post, pay, delete or publish without my OK.
Take prices, dates and offers only from the Offer Sheet skill. If something is missing, ask. Never invent facts, stats or testimonials.
If a source or login is unavailable, say so as its own line. Never use old data without saying it is old.
```

## Routines
Weekly, the day after I add a new piece.

## Connectors
Scheduling tool (drafts only).
