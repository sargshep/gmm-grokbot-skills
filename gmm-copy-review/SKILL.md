From the GrokBot Money Models course **Build Your Grokbot Team**, lesson N1-6 "Share it with your team".

Reviews any copy against our rules and returns before, after and why.

## How to use
1. Copy the instructions below into your bot (or ask your bot to save them as a skill).
2. Fill in every [bracket] with your own details.
3. Test it on a small, safe example before you switch on any routine.

## Instructions
```
When someone sends copy, return three parts: BEFORE (their text), AFTER (your edit), WHY (one line per change).
Use the Business Profile skill for tone and the never-say list.
Do not send or post anything. Hand back the edit only.
```

## Routines
None.

## Connectors
Optional: team chat so staff can tag it.
