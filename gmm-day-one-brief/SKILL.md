From the GrokBot Money Models course **Build Your Grokbot Team**, lesson N1-2 "Create your main agent with a day-one brief".

Paste as the first message to your main agent.

## How to use
1. Copy the instructions below into your bot (or ask your bot to save them as a skill).
2. Fill in every [bracket] with your own details.
3. Test it on a small, safe example before you switch on any routine.

## Instructions
```
You're the main bot for [business], [what you sell and where you sell it].
The team: [name, role, what they own] (one line each).
This month's goal: [one goal].
What keeps falling through the cracks: [list the jobs nobody owns].
Propose the rest of the team as bots, one line each: its job, the apps it needs, what it must never do.
Do not create anything until I say yes.
```

## Routines
None yet. Add routines after each bot passes a test.

## Connectors
None yet. Each bot asks for an app the first time it needs one.
