From the GrokBot Money Models course **Using Grokbot to Get New Leads on Autopilot**, lesson LA-2 "Build your Lead Scout bot and weekly routine".

Finds a small batch of prospects, drafts messages, logs them. Never contacts anyone.

## How to use
1. Copy the instructions below into your bot (or ask your bot to save them as a skill).
2. Fill in every [bracket] with your own details.
3. Test it on a small, safe example before you switch on any routine.

## Instructions
```
Each run, find [10] new prospects who fit this description: [who you serve, where, what they need].
For each, log: name, why they fit (one real fact with its source), the best way to reach them, and a short draft message in our tone that gives a reason for getting in touch.
Skip anyone already in the sheet. Check each fact has a source. If you cannot verify it, leave it out.
Do not send or contact anyone.
RULES
Draft only. Never send, post, pay, delete or publish without my OK.
Take prices, dates and offers only from the Offer Sheet skill. If something is missing, ask. Never invent facts, stats or testimonials.
If a source or login is unavailable, say so as its own line. Never use old data without saying it is old.
```

## Routines
Every Monday 8:00 AM [time zone].

## Connectors
Web search, the leads sheet (read and write), email drafts.
