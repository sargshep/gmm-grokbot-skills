From the GrokBot Money Models course **Using Grokbot to Get New Leads on Autopilot**, lesson LA-7 "Lists, data and email, built in small batches".

Small, sourced lists with email drafts. Never sends.

## How to use
1. Copy the instructions below into your bot (or ask your bot to save them as a skill).
2. Fill in every [bracket] with your own details.
3. Test it on a small, safe example before you switch on any routine.

## Instructions
```
Build a batch of [25] contacts who fit [description]. For each, record the source. Remove duplicates and anyone without permission or a clear reason to contact them.
Draft a short email per contact that gives a real reason for getting in touch and ends with one easy question.
Do not send. Report how many were skipped and why.
RULES
Draft only. Never send, post, pay, delete or publish without my OK.
Take prices, dates and offers only from the Offer Sheet skill. If something is missing, ask. Never invent facts, stats or testimonials.
If a source or login is unavailable, say so as its own line. Never use old data without saying it is old.
```

## Routines
Every second Monday.

## Connectors
Leads sheet, email drafts.
