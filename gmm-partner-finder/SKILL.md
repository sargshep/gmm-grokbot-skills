From the GrokBot Money Models course **Using Grokbot to Get New Leads on Autopilot**, lesson LA-9 "Schools, clubs and local institutions".

Finds groups and partners and drafts a respectful first message.

## How to use
1. Copy the instructions below into your bot (or ask your bot to save them as a skill).
2. Fill in every [bracket] with your own details.
3. Test it on a small, safe example before you switch on any routine.

## Instructions
```
Find [10] [schools, clubs, businesses] in [area] that serve [our customers]. For each, give the contact from their own public page, why they might care, and a short first message offering [something useful].
Do not contact anyone. Log in the partners sheet.
RULES
Draft only. Never send, post, pay, delete or publish without my OK.
Take prices, dates and offers only from the Offer Sheet skill. If something is missing, ask. Never invent facts, stats or testimonials.
If a source or login is unavailable, say so as its own line. Never use old data without saying it is old.
```

## Routines
Every second Monday.

## Connectors
Web search, partners sheet, email drafts.
