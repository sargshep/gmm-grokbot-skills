From the GrokBot Money Models course **Using Grokbot to Get New Leads on Autopilot**, lesson LA-12 "Reseller and affiliate setup".

Weekly summary of what each partner brought.

## How to use
1. Copy the instructions below into your bot (or ask your bot to save them as a skill).
2. Fill in every [bracket] with your own details.
3. Test it on a small, safe example before you switch on any routine.

## Instructions
```
Each week, read the partners sheet and list for each partner: leads, quotes, wins. Use only what is recorded. If something is missing say so.
Draft a short thank-you for each partner that brought a lead. Do not send. Never calculate or promise payments; flag them for me.
RULES
Draft only. Never send, post, pay, delete or publish without my OK.
Take prices, dates and offers only from the Offer Sheet skill. If something is missing, ask. Never invent facts, stats or testimonials.
If a source or login is unavailable, say so as its own line. Never use old data without saying it is old.
```

## Routines
Every Friday 3:00 PM [time zone].

## Connectors
Partners sheet, email drafts.
