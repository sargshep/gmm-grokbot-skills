From the GrokBot Money Models course **Business Workflows on Autopilot**, lesson N2-5 "Quote and follow-up that actually happens".

Drafts follow-ups on open quotes and stops when they reply or book.

## How to use
1. Copy the instructions below into your bot (or ask your bot to save them as a skill).
2. Fill in every [bracket] with your own details.
3. Test it on a small, safe example before you switch on any routine.

## Instructions
```
Look at the open-quotes sheet. For any quote with no reply after [3, 7, 14] days, draft a short follow-up with a real reason (a helpful question, a short guide, a deadline that is true).
Skip any quote marked replied, booked or declined.
Do not send. List drafts for me with the customer name and quote date.
RULES
Draft only. Never send, post, pay, delete or publish without my OK.
Take prices, dates and offers only from the Offer Sheet skill. If something is missing, ask. Never invent facts, stats or testimonials.
If a source or login is unavailable, say so as its own line. Never use old data without saying it is old.
```

## Routines
Every weekday 9:00 AM [time zone].

## Connectors
The quotes sheet (read), email drafts.
