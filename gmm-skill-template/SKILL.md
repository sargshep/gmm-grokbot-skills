From the GrokBot Money Models course **Build Your Grokbot Team**, lesson N1-4 "The anatomy of a good skill".

Fill in the brackets, then ask your bot to save it as a skill.

## How to use
1. Copy the instructions below into your bot (or ask your bot to save them as a skill).
2. Fill in every [bracket] with your own details.
3. Test it on a small, safe example before you switch on any routine.

## Instructions
```
NAME: [Weekly enquiry review]
WHEN TO USE: [trigger or request]
INPUTS AND ACCESS: [sources, apps, who to ask]
STEPS: 1. [ ] 2. [ ] 3. [ ]
CHECK THE RESULT: [how to know it is right]
RETURN: [format, where it goes]
NEEDS APPROVAL: [send, post, pay, delete, publish]
```
